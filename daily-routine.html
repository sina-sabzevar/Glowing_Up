The week

DayTrainingNight skin

The week starts on Saturday, with Friday as the full rest day. Gym days are never back-to-back for the same muscles.

Rules that make the day work

  • Laptop off the floor. In your photos it sits on the floor mattress. Put it on a table or stand at eye level, with a separate keyboard. This matters more than any single exercise.
  • Two 1.5 L bottles. Finish one by 13:00 and the other by 19:00. Then you never have to count glasses.
  • Phone alarm every 2 hours labelled "ribs down, chin back, glutes on".
  • Pack tomorrow tonight: gym bag, lunch box and a clean shirt. Mornings are then only follow-through.
  • Miss one item, not two. A skipped walk is fine. Two skipped days in a row is where plans die.

What the photos show, and what each fix targets

  • Side view: belly pushed forward with an arched lower back. Targeted by the deficit, daily walking, glute work and the hip-flexor stretch.
  • Shoulders and head: slightly forward. Targeted by rows, face pulls, pull-aparts and chin tucks.
  • Back: scattered small dark marks from old bumps. Targeted by the benzoyl peroxide wash each morning, azelaic acid at night, no picking and sunscreen on skin that sees sun.
  • Face: dark under-eyes, a reddish mark on the left cheek, visible pores on the nose. Targeted by 7.5–8 h sleep, SPF 50, niacinamide, retinol and azelaic acid.

Posture work helps how you stand and look, but results vary between people. Expect gradual change over months rather than a fixed number of centimetres.

Additions to the shopping list

  • Azelaic acid 10–20% cream or gel from a pharmacy (آزلائیک اسید). Use it on the back marks and on the cheek mark.
  • White towels and a few white T-shirts to sleep in. Benzoyl peroxide bleaches coloured fabric.
  • Two 1.5 L water bottles
  • Laptop stand or a table and a cheap separate keyboard
  • Check every brand from the original list on Digikala or at a pharmacy before buying. Some names in the PDF may be wrong.

This is general wellness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before a new diet or training plan. If the under-eye darkness doesn't improve after a month of good sleep, ask them about iron and allergies. See a dermatologist for any mark that is raised, pitted, changing, or not fading after 3 months. Creatine usually adds 1–2 kg of water weight in the first 2–3 weeks. That is not fat, so judge fat loss from week 4 onward and from the waist measurement.
